# Pricefinder

> Australian property intelligence and valuation platform (pricefinder.com.au), a brand of Domain Holdings Australia — acquired by CoStar Group in August 2025. Supplies property records, comparable sales and rental history, suburb statistics, AVM price estimates and CMA/appraisal reports to real estate agencies, mortgage brokers, banks and lenders, valuers, property developers, advisory firms and government. Blends Domain and Allhomes first-party listing feeds with state and territory government land valuation and sales data.

## What an agent needs to know first

- **The contract is public; the data is not.** A complete Swagger 2.0 document (v1.13.1, 112 paths, 116 operations, 188 definitions, 19 tags) and a live Swagger UI are served anonymously from api.pricefinder.com.au. Every data path returns **401** without a token.
- **There is no self-serve signup, no free tier, no sandbox and no trial key.** Credentials are issued only under a sales-negotiated commercial subscription with a minimum one-month term, governed by the Domain Group API Terms and Conditions.
- **Auth is OAuth 2.0** — client_credentials, authorization_code (hosted authorize page) and refresh_token — but the contract declares **no securityDefinitions**; the whole model is HTML prose inside the `POST /oauth2/token` description.
- **No OAuth scopes exist.** Entitlement is discovered at runtime via `GET /features` (operationId `getFeatures`, schema `UserFeatures`). Call it first.
- **Read-mostly.** 109 of 116 operations are reads. There is no write path into properties, listings, sales or valuations, and no payment or settlement surface.
- **No pagination, no idempotency, no rate-limit signalling, no request-id, no changelog, no status page, no webhooks.**
- **Read `messages[]` on every 200.** Data-quality and jurisdictional-suppression notices ride inside success payloads; a 200 does not mean a complete answer.

## APIs

- [Pricefinder Property API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): property record retrieval — core and extended detail, images, floorplans, street view, maps, schools, radial searches for nearby sales/rentals/listings/properties/development applications, and PDF property reports. 22 operations.
- [Pricefinder AVM & Valuation API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): automated valuation output as JSON (`GET /properties/{propertyId}/avm`) and as PDF (`/avm/pdf`), plus auto-CMA sale and rental PDFs.
- [Pricefinder Sales, Rentals & Listings API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): individual sale/rental/listing retrieval by id, and enumeration scoped by suburb, postcode, street or spatial boundary.
- [Pricefinder Suburb & Market Statistics API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): suburb detail and summary, demographics, flyover reports and PDFs, street enumeration, peak selling periods, sale price segmentation, and time series. 15 operations.
- [Pricefinder Title & Land Reference API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): per-state resolution of Australian land title references to Pricefinder property records across NSW, VIC, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, NT and ACT. 25 operations — the land-registry seam expressed as an API.
- [Pricefinder Search & Suggest API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): address, street, suburb and typeahead suggestion, lon/lat reverse suggestion, owner-name search, and spatial queries.
- [Pricefinder Appraisals & CMA API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): sales CMA, rental CMA and statement-of-information retrieval by appraisal share id, appraisal listing and insights, agent/office/logo imagery.
- [Pricefinder Property Event Subscriptions API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): subscribe/list/delete alerts for ForSale, ForRent, Sold and SoldVerified. **Email delivery only — not webhooks.**
- [Pricefinder SSO API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): deep links handing an authenticated user into the Pricefinder web app at a specific property, CMA, appraisal, SOI, radial-sales or titles context.
- [Pricefinder OAuth 2.0 Token API](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): `POST /oauth2/token` for client_credentials, authorization_code and refresh_token grants.

## Client libraries

- [`GET /v1/stubs/{language}`](https://api.pricefinder.com.au/v1/swagger/index.html): first-party swagger-codegen client-library generator across 38 target languages. Auth-gated (401 anonymous) and explicitly unsupported — the contract states "Pricefinder is unable to provide support for this endpoint … There is no guarantee of backwards compatibility."
- No published, supported SDK exists on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, Packagist, crates.io, Maven Central, NuGet or pkg.go.dev under Pricefinder or Domain Group. The `pricefinder` RubyGem and the `domain-pricefinder` npm packages are **third-party**.

## Optional

- Australia has **no MLS and no RESO regime**. No RESO Web API certification, no Data Dictionary certification, no OData `$metadata` document and no Universal Property Identifier appear anywhere in Pricefinder's contract or documentation. Identity is a proprietary integer `propertyId`, joined to land title through 25 bespoke per-state lookup paths.
- Nothing Pricefinder serves is open data. Underlying government sources may be open in their own right, but Pricefinder republishes them only under a paid licence, and ownership detail is restricted ("Available for WA, QLD, NSW, VIC. Restrictions apply.").
- Sale price and sale date are wrapper types (`SensitivePrice`, `SensitiveDate`) that may be legally withheld — the data model encodes Australian jurisdictional disclosure law directly. Every entity carries a `disclaimer` field, so licensing text travels with the payload.
